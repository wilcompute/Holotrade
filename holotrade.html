Optimal reservation shapes

exact · every witness attains its spectral bound
The spectral bounds say how dense a reservation of a given size could be. A scheduler cannot reserve a bound — it reserves a set. These are the sets that actually attain the bounds, found by exhaustive search and re-checked independently by eigenspace projection. A densest shape exists at every multiple of four and nowhere else; the four-point ones are exactly the 40 totally isotropic lines.
“Survives” is a worst-case guarantee, not an average. It is one less than the minimum number of blocked points that defeats every placement of the shape — so with that many nodes busy a placement is certain, whichever nodes they are. Proved two independent ways: branch-and-bound, and SAT with every smaller size discharged as UNSAT. The four-node figure of 10 is exactly the no-ovoid result in disguise: ten points could only block all 40 lines by being an ovoid, and there is none.

How the wear model treats dispersion

Model assumption. The seeded wear function penalises utilisation swings more than steady load at the same average. Smoother load therefore accumulates less modelled wear and spreads modelled service events. HoloTrade has not measured a hardware lifetime improvement.

Assets & UOR

Each asset carries explicit delivery policies. The dashboard turns documented policy defaults into an estimated market-breadth score across forty reference regions; it is not observed liquidity or a group-theoretic co-volume.

The address

2⁶⁴ = 40 × 1,296 × payload
The forty vertices of W(3,3) are in canonical bijection with the forty Sylow-3 subgroups of Sp(4,𝔽₃), so an object reference is a pair (P, x): a Sylow subgroup and a coset representative. 40 × 1,296 = 51,840 = |Aut(W(3,3))|. An asset's identity and its position in the fabric are the same 64 bits — there is no separate registry mapping ids to locations that can go stale, and no lookup to do.

Policy-estimated market breadth

configured defaults · not tape liquidity
Each default estimates the fraction of forty reference delivery regions in which an asset remains eligible. Multiple policies use the tightest score, avoiding an invented independence assumption. These are configurable model inputs unless a venue supplies measured eligibility.
