# Correct heterotic target for the W33 outer inversion

The cyclic Z6-II point group is the wrong extension: its C2 commutes with its C3. The literature contains the right non-Abelian class. Konopka's explicit heterotic S3 orbifold uses generators tau (order 2) and sigma (order 3) with

`tau^2 = sigma^3 = (tau sigma)^2 = 1`, hence `tau sigma tau^-1 = sigma^-1`.

This is exactly the group law of the certified W33 pair `s z s^-1 = z^-1`. Thus the abstract extension-level bridge is exact under tau->s and sigma->z. The physical representations are still different objects (geometric point-group rotation versus Heisenberg center phase), so a representation/gauge-spectrum intertwiner remains open.
