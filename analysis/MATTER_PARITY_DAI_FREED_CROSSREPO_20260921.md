The exact W33 charge split 27=16_1+10_-2+1_4 gives matter parity P_M=(-1)^Qpsi. The parent anomaly sums vanish:

16-20+4=0,
16-80+64=0,
2-2=0.

The first two are the mixed gravitational and cubic U(1)psi coefficients; the third is SO(10)^2 U(1)psi with T(16)=2 and T(10)=1.

This is not the twisted Z4_X symmetry whose generator squares to fermion parity and whose anomaly can be Z16.
