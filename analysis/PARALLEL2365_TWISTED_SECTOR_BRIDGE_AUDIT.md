# Audit of the parallel twisted-sector bridge

The parallel commit `2365e596` contains a valuable insight but one statement is too strong.

The literature supports the **sector-label** dictionary: the \(\vartheta\) and \(\vartheta^2\) twisted sectors have opposite order-three selection-rule charges and transform in conjugate flavor representations. Their two-dimensional sector label can therefore carry the same normal form as the W33 logical doublet,
\[
z=\operatorname{diag}(\omega,\omega^{-1}),\qquad X_L=\text{swap}.
\]

However the full W33 carrier uses \(H=3^{1+12}\), so each faithful Schrödinger sector has dimension \(3^6=729\). A factorized six-real-dimensional heterotic \(T^6/\mathbb Z_3\) has at most three complex \(T^2/\mathbb Z_3\) fixed-point factors, giving the shared-center fixed-point Heisenberg group \(3^{1+6}\) and faithful irreps of dimension only \(3^3=27\).

Thus ordinary fixed-point flavor alone gives at most a \(27\oplus27\) internal module, not \(729\oplus729\). The exact bridge presently closed is the **C² central-character/twisted-sector label**. A full 1458-state heterotic realization needs three additional ternary canonical pairs, plausibly from gauge/Narain/oscillator data, with a proved common central extension.

A second distinction is essential: the geometric \(S_3\) permutation symmetry of an Abelian \(\mathbb Z_3\) orbifold need not itself be an orbifold projection generator. In Konopka's genuinely non-Abelian \(S_3\) point-group compactification it is part of the point group and does project; CP-like Narain outer automorphisms instead constrain the theory without becoming the original orbifold quotient.
